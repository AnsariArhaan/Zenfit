About ZENFIT

Built by lifters, for lifters.

ZENFIT started as a class project and turned into something we actually wanted to use — a single place to plan a session, track a number, and remember why you started. No fluff, no locked-behind-paywall macros, just the tools we wished existed on day one.

Why "ZENFIT"?

A pulse is honest — it speeds up when you push and settles when you rest. That's the whole philosophy: train hard, track it honestly, recover on purpose.

ZENFIT — student project prototype · built for better habits, not just better numbers.
